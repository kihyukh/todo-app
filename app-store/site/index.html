Make a little progress

Set one or several days to work on a task, separately from its final deadline. Today keeps the work you chose close at hand.

Keep the context

Write notes with Markdown, equations, checklists, images, and documents. The details stay alongside the task they belong to.

See how the day fits

View your Apple Calendar accounts beside your tasks, and link work to the events it supports. Calendar access is optional.

Keep your own workspace

Work offline or choose a shared folder in iCloud Drive for your devices. No GreenDay account, advertising, or analytics SDK.
